Who We Are

D & C – AgriBase (Practical Knowledge. Personalised Advice. Better Farming Decisions.) is an agricultural consultancy and farm advisory service operating alongside the AgriBase digital platform and the Farmer Community App. Whether you are growing maize, sorghum, tomatoes, or other crops, raising livestock, or planning a new agricultural enterprise, AgriBase helps you make more informed farming decisions.

Our Mission

To make practical, reliable, and accessible agricultural knowledge available to farmers through online consultations, farm visits, agricultural training, digital tools, and personalised farming advice.

Our Vision

To become a trusted agricultural consultancy connecting farmers with practical agricultural expertise and modern farming technology to support better farm management, productivity, sustainability, and profitability.

Our Core Values

Professionalism Rigorous technical discipline and respectful grower communication.
Practical Farming Knowledge Agronomy grounded in real soil conditions, water availability, and cost constraints.
Honesty & Transparency Clear pricing, frank risk assessments, and zero hidden commercial bias.
Responsible Recommendations Integrated pest management and sustainable practices that safeguard land fertility.
Leadership & Principal Consultant

Enock Chinatsvi

Founder & Principal Agricultural Consultant

Enock Chinatsvi holds a Diploma in Agriculture from Chibhero Agricultural College in Zimbabwe, where he studied from 2015 to 2018. Combining formal agronomic education with hands-on field experience, Enock has advised growers across horticulture, maize, legumes, irrigation planning, plant protection, and farm business management.

In addition to field consultancy, Enock is the lead developer behind the AgriBase platform and the Farmer Community App, creating accessible digital calculators and field manuals used by thousands of agricultural producers across the continent.

Consultancy Team Structure

D & C – AgriBase operates with an agile, highly responsive operational structure designed to serve farmers efficiently:

  • Role 1: Founder & Principal Consultant (Enock Chinatsvi): Responsible for agronomic diagnostic reviews, on-farm physical inspections, technical advisory reports, soil & fertiliser programmes, and quality assurance.
  • Role 2: Client Services & Operations Assistant: Coordinates booking schedules, facilitates farmer enquiry communications, prepares initial quotation drafts, issues appointment reminders, and manages administrative records.
