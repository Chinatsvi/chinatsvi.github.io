About AgriBase

Practical knowledge for better farm decisions

AgriBase brings together farming guides, calculators, crop planning information, educational content and the Farmer Community app.

Who we are

AgriBase is an agricultural information and tools project focused on useful, understandable resources for farmers, especially across Africa and Southern Africa.

Why it was created

Farm decisions often require a mix of local knowledge, records, weather observation and careful calculations. This website makes those starting points easier to find while acknowledging that conditions differ from farm to farm.

Our approach

  • Explain assumptions instead of hiding them.
  • Connect guides, tools, crop planning and learning.
  • Avoid unsupported claims and universal recommendations.
  • Keep the community app and public website connected.
