About AgriBase

AgriBase: The Agricultural Platform Powering the Farmer Community App

AgriBase brings together practical farming guides, calculators, crop planning information, educational content, and the Farmer Community App under one agricultural platform.

Who we are

AgriBase is the agricultural platform behind the Farmer Community App, delivering practical farming information and decision-support tools to farmers across Africa and beyond.

Why it was created

Farm decisions often require a mix of local knowledge, records, weather observation, and careful calculations. AgriBase makes those starting points easier to access while acknowledging that conditions differ from farm to farm.

Our approach

  • Explain assumptions instead of hiding them.
  • Connect guides, tools, crop planning, and learning.
  • Avoid unsupported claims and universal recommendations.
  • Keep the Farmer Community App and the AgriBase platform closely connected.
