About me

An agronomist who codes

I'm Enock Chinatsvi, an agronomist and Flutter developer from Zimbabwe. I saw farmers making costly decisions without easy access to the information that could have prevented them, so I started building tools to close that gap.

AgriBase is the result: a farming knowledge site and companion app, built with input from the farmers who use it, and kept free so cost is never the reason someone goes without it.
