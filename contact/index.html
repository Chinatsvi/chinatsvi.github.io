Frequently Asked Questions

How soon will I receive a reply to my enquiry?

We respond to all submitted farming questions and booking requests within 24 to 48 business hours. For emergency pest outbreaks, message our direct WhatsApp line with the reference code.

Do you charge for asking a simple farming question?

Our single farming question review service is currently provided free of charge as part of our commitment to accessible agricultural knowledge. Detailed whole-farm visits, custom fertilizer programmes, and multi-hour consultations are quoted transparently.

How are farm visit travel costs calculated?

Travel fees are calculated using standard estimated round-trip road distance from our operational hub at an owner-configured mileage rate (e.g. $0.65/km). You will receive this itemized in a written quotation before any commitment is made.
