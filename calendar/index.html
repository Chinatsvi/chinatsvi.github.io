Key Seasonal Planning Stages

  • Land Preparation & Soil Testing: Prepare friable soil beds, measure pH balance, and apply basal fertilizer 2–4 weeks prior to planting.
  • Planting Window Alignment: Match transplanting or sowing dates to frost-free thermal windows and optimal seasonal rainfall cycles.
  • Growth Stage Scouting & Weeding: Conduct weekly field scouting for early pest symptoms (e.g. cutworms, aphids, leafminers) and manage weed competition early.
  • Targeted Split Fertilization: Apply top-dressings (CAN/Potassium) aligned with vegetative canopy build and fruit bulking stages.
  • Irrigation Scheduling: Adjust water delivery during moisture-sensitive stages (e.g. flowering, silking, bulking) to prevent fruit splitting or flower drop.
  • Harvest Window & Market Transport: Harvest at peak commercial maturity (breaker stage for long transport or full ripe for local markets) and shade crates immediately.
