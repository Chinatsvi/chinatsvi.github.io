D & C – AgriBase provides agronomic guidance, farm inspections, diagnostic opinions, irrigation audits, and technical training. Recommendations are formulated based on information, soil symptoms, photographs, or field observations provided by the farmer or observed during inspection.

When appropriate, our consultants will advise you to obtain certified laboratory testing.
