About D & C – AgriBase

Practical Farming Knowledge. Professional Advice. Better Farming Decisions.

D & C – AgriBase was founded to bridge the gap between academic agronomy and everyday field realities. Whether you are managing five hectares of horticulture, a commercial maize stand, or starting a livestock enterprise, we provide field-tested recommendations tailored to your exact soil, water, and capital conditions.

We work directly alongside growers to diagnose problems early, calibrate inputs accurately, reduce unnecessary chemical expenditure, and build resilient, profitable farm operations.
