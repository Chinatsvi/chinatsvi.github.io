Livestock Feed Planner

Feed planning depends on species, age, body size, pregnancy, production stage, forage quality, water and local ingredients. Start by recording animals, available forage and purchased feed.

  • Record feed quantity, cost and storage condition.
  • Change rations gradually and observe intake and body condition.
  • Provide clean water and safe mineral access where advised.
  • Use qualified livestock or nutrition advice for formulated rations.
