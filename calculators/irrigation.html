Irrigation Calculator

Estimate a crop water requirement from reference ETo, crop coefficient, effective rainfall and system efficiency.

Method: ETc = ETo x Kc; net demand = ETc - rainfall; gross depth = net demand / efficiency. This page estimates one day.

The app supports crop stages, intervals, live weather fallback and multiple systems. This public estimate should be checked against soil moisture, rainfall, measured system output and local guidance.
